      About Me

      Dedicated and patient-focused physiotherapist with 3 years of clinical experience and a Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) degree, with clinical exposure across musculoskeletal, neurological, cardiopulmonary, pediatric, and general rehabilitation settings. Skilled in patient assessment, range of motion (ROM) assessment, therapeutic exercises, muscle strengthening, mobility training, and functional rehabilitation. Brings a strong foundation in rehabilitation principles, patient care, and evidence-based treatment approaches, with a commitment to continuous learning and to improving patient outcomes. Focused on delivering patient-centered care, supporting functional recovery, and promoting patient independence.
